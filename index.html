How to Play

1. Choose how you want to play: The facilitator clicks the “Play in Teams” or “Play Individually” button and picks the team or player count. The facilitator also clicks the “View Question & Answer Bank” button to view or “Print Question & Answer Bank” button to print questions and answers, then uses this bank to confirm answers provided in the game.

2. Choose a dollar amount: The player or team whose turn it is chooses an available dollar amount on the game board ($100, $200, $300, $400, or $500). The facilitator then clicks that dollar amount on the game board to open and reveal the clue, reads it aloud, and uses the Question & Answer Bank to check the correct answer.

3. Answer the clue and award points: Any player or team may call out an answer. Once someone responds, the facilitator clicks the “Reveal Answer” button, then clicks the button for the applicable player or team in the clue box to award them the points for a correct answer. That player or team then chooses the next dollar amount.

4. Continue playing: If no one knows the answer, the facilitator can reveal it without awarding points, and play continues with another dollar amount. The player or team with the most money at the end wins. The facilitator can end the game after a preferred amount of time or when all dollar amounts are picked.

Note: Player or team names can be changed by clicking the name box for each player or team and entering a new name.

Educational activity. Facts are based on public-health and scientific sources. Select “View source” on each clue to explore the research behind the answer.
